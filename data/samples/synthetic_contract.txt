MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. Payment Terms
The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days after receipt.

2. Statement of Work Payment
For services under this Statement of Work, invoices shall be payable within sixty (60) days after receipt and approval.

3. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving thirty (30) days written notice.

4. Project Termination
The Customer may terminate the applicable Statement of Work on fifteen (15) days written notice if the project is delayed.
